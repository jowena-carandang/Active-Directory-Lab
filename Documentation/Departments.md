# 🏢 Departments

## Overview

Futurion Systems currently employs **35 staff members** across seven departments. As a growing managed IT services, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity provider, the company relies on close collaboration between business and technical teams to deliver secure, reliable, and scalable technology solutions.

The **Technology Services** department supports both Futurion Systems' internal infrastructure and managed IT environments for client organizations. This portfolio documents the design and implementation of the company's internal enterprise environment, following industry best practices used by modern Managed Service Providers (MSPs).

The organizational structure below serves as the foundation for the Active Directory environment and will be referenced throughout this portfolio when creating Organizational Units (OUs), security groups, user accounts, shared folders, and Group Policy Objects (GPOs).

# Department Details

## Executive Management

The Executive Management team is responsible for defining the company's strategic direction, overseeing business operations, approving technology investments, and ensuring sustainable growth.

**Typical Roles**

* Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
* Operations Director

---

## Technology Services

The Technology Services department is responsible for designing, implementing, securing, and maintaining both Futurion Systems' internal IT infrastructure and the managed environments of client organizations.

The team delivers managed IT services, cloud solutions, cybersecurity, infrastructure administration, technical support, and proactive monitoring to ensure business continuity and operational efficiency.

**Typical Roles**

* Systems Administrator
* Cloud Engineer
* Cybersecurity Analyst
* Helpdesk Technician (Level 1)
* Helpdesk Technician (Level 2)
* IT Support Officer

---

## Sales

The Sales department develops relationships with prospective clients, manages customer accounts, prepares service proposals, and works closely with Technology Services to understand client requirements before projects begin.

**Typical Roles**

* Sales Manager
* Senior Account Executive
* Business Development Representative
* Sales Consultant

---

## Marketing

The Marketing department promotes the company's managed services through digital campaigns, branding initiatives, website management, educational content, and social media engagement.

**Typical Roles**

* Marketing Manager
* Digital Marketing Specialist
* Graphic Designer
* Content Coordinator
* Social Media Coordinator

---

## Operations

The Operations department coordinates project delivery, manages service schedules, oversees internal workflows, and ensures client projects are completed efficiently and consistently.

**Typical Roles**

* Operations Coordinator
* Project Support Officer
* Service Delivery Specialist
* Customer Success Representative

---

## Finance

The Finance department manages budgeting, payroll, invoicing, procurement, financial reporting, and compliance to support the company's day-to-day operations.

**Typical Roles**

* Finance Manager
* Accountant
* Payroll Officer

---

## Human Resources

The Human Resources department manages recruitment, onboarding, employee records, workplace policies, compliance, and professional development to support a productive and positive work environment.

**Typical Roles**

* HR Manager
* Recruitment Officer
* HR Administrator

---

# Active Directory Integration

Each department will receive:

* A dedicated Organizational Unit (OU)
* Department-specific security groups
* Standardized user and computer naming conventions
* Departmental shared folders with role-based permissions
* Group Policy Objects (GPOs) tailored to departmental requirements
* Centralized authentication and access management

This structure provides secure identity management, simplifies administration, and creates a scalable Microsoft Active Directory environment capable of supporting both the company's continued growth and future cloud integration with Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Entra ID.
