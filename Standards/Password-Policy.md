# Password Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the password requirements for all user accounts within the Futurion Systems Active Directory environment.

---

## Password Requirements

Minimum Length:
12 characters

Complexity:
Must contain at least three of the following:

- Uppercase letters
- Lowercase letters
- Numbers
- Special characters

Example:

Futurion@2026!

---

## Password Expiration

Maximum Password Age:
90 Days

Minimum Password Age:
1 Day

Password History:
Remember last 24 passwords

---

## Account Lockout

Failed Login Attempts:
5

Lockout Duration:
30 Minutes

Reset Counter:
30 Minutes

---

## Administrator Accounts

Administrator accounts must:

- Use unique passwords
- Never share credentials
- Use least privilege
- Be separate from standard user accounts
